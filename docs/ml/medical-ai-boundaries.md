# Medical AI Boundaries & Clinical Disclaimer Governance

**System**: LiverAI Precision Diagnostics & Analytics  
**Regulatory Class**: Clinical Decision Support / Health Information Tool (Non-Diagnostic)  

---

## 1. Intended Use & Target Audience
LiverAI is designed as an educational, screening, and decision-support assistant for liver health monitoring. It evaluates 10 standardized clinical blood biomarkers (LPD dataset) and histology tissue patch images to generate risk indicators.

## 2. Clinical Boundaries & Non-Diagnostic Scope
- **Non-Diagnostic**: Predictions generated by the 10-biomarker LPD model or EfficientNet-B0 histology vision classifier do **not** constitute a definitive clinical diagnosis.
- **Human-in-the-Loop**: All outputs must be interpreted by qualified healthcare professionals (gastroenterologists, hepatologists, or general practitioners).

## 3. Disclaimers & Patient Safety Disclosures
- Every diagnostic report, chat consultation, and PDF export includes mandatory medical disclaimers:
  > *"Notice: LiverAI outputs provide clinical risk indicators for educational and screening guidance only. Please consult a physician for diagnostic evaluation and personalized treatment plans."*

## 4. Production Mode Fail-Closed Policy
- When running in `APP_ENV=production`, mock or synthetic simulation modes are strictly prohibited (`ENABLE_VISION_SIMULATION=false`).
- If vision model weights or dependencies are unavailable, the backend fails closed with `503 MODEL_UNAVAILABLE` rather than generating false or simulated diagnostics.
