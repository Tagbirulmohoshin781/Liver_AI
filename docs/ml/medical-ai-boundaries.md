# LiverAI Medical AI Boundaries & Ethical Framework

## Intended Purpose
LiverAI is designed exclusively for **educational, informational, and research support** purposes. It is **not a diagnostic medical device** and has not received regulatory clearance (such as FDA 510(k) or CE mark) for clinical decision making.

## Component Classifications

| Component | Status | Intended Use | Prohibited Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| RAG Dialogue Engine | Grounded Educational Assistant | General liver health education grounded in AASLD/CDC reference texts. | Prescribing medication, providing primary diagnoses. |
| LPD Clinical Risk Calculator | Experimental Heuristic | Visualizing reference range deviations according to standard laboratory liver panels. | Clinical risk stratification, diagnostic certainty. |
| Biopsy Histology Classifier | Research / Experimental Model | Research exploration of cellular patterns (steatosis, fibrosis, ballooning, inflammation). | Guiding surgical or pharmaceutical intervention without pathologist review. |
