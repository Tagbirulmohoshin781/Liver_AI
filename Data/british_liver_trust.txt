TITLE: British Liver Trust — Liver Health, Diet, Alcohol & Lifestyle Guidance
SOURCE: https://liveruk.org/
AUTHORITY: British Liver Trust (Evidence Level 2A)
CLINICAL DOMAIN: Nutrition, Alcohol Guidelines, Liver Support

KEY GUIDELINES FROM BRITISH LIVER TRUST:
1. Alcohol Policy: There is no safe drinking limit for anyone with existing liver inflammation, fatty liver, or fibrosis. Complete abstinence is recommended to prevent progression to cirrhosis.
2. Coffee Consumption: Research supported by British Liver Trust demonstrates that drinking 2–3 cups of filtered black coffee daily protects against liver damage, reduces fibrosis progression, and lowers the risk of liver cancer.
3. Mediterranean Diet: Base meals around vegetables, fruit, beans, pulses, nuts, seeds, whole grains, and healthy fats (olive oil). Minimize red and processed meat, sugary foods, and refined carbohydrates.
4. Hydration & Sugar: Drink 2–3 litres of water daily. Avoid sugary fizzy drinks, concentrated fruit juices, and foods high in fructose which directly fuel fat deposition in liver cells.
