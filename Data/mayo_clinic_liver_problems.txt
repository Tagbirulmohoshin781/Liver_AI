TITLE: Mayo Clinic — Liver Disease Symptoms, Causes & Complications
SOURCE: https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/liver-problems/symptoms-causes/syc-20374502
AUTHORITY: Mayo Clinic Division of Gastroenterology and Hepatology (Evidence Level 1B)
CLINICAL DOMAIN: Liver Problems, Fibrosis Progression, Clinical Complications

CLINICAL OVERVIEW:
Liver disease doesn't always cause noticeable signs and symptoms. When symptoms occur, they reflect impaired synthetic function, portal hypertension, or biliary obstruction.

KEY SIGNS AND SYMPTOMS:
- Skin and eyes that appear yellowish (jaundice).
- Abdominal pain and swelling, particularly in the right upper quadrant.
- Swelling in the legs and ankles (peripheral edema).
- Itchy skin (pruritus due to bile salt deposition).
- Dark urine color and pale stool color.
- Chronic fatigue, nausea or vomiting, loss of appetite.
- Tendency to bruise easily (coagulopathy from impaired clotting factor synthesis).

CAUSES & RISK FACTORS:
- Infection: Parasites and viruses causing Hepatitis A, B, and C.
- Immune system abnormality: Autoimmune hepatitis, PBC, PSC.
- Genetics: Abnormal genes inherited from parents leading to iron or copper accumulation.
- Cancer and growths: Liver cancer, bile duct cancer, liver adenoma.
- Toxic & Metabolic: Heavy alcohol use, obesity, Type 2 diabetes, high cholesterol, exposure to industrial toxins.

COMPLICATIONS:
Untreated chronic liver inflammation leads to progressive fibrosis (scarring), bridging fibrosis, cirrhosis, portal hypertension, esophageal varices hemorrhage, and liver failure.
